racomi bundles these third-party Agent icons:

  src/assets/agents/claude.svg  (claudecode-color.svg)
  src/assets/agents/codex.svg   (codex-color.svg)
  src/assets/agents/cursor.svg  (cursor.svg)

The SVG files are adapted from Magpie's GUI assets at commit
612b759d2141cccb4f477645c9d765c0878476b0:
https://github.com/yetone/magpie/tree/612b759d2141cccb4f477645c9d765c0878476b0/internal/gui/assets/icons

These brand icons originate from Lobe Icons:
https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons

The respective trademarks and logos belong to their owners. Their use here
identifies the supported Agent applications.

The navigation layouts, theme tokens, compact controls, and feedback styles
also adapt Magpie's GUI implementation. Sidebar navigation, layout switching,
and resizing were referenced from main at commit
74b30cb7f86763d9e7cc4574477379b939487fd4:
https://github.com/yetone/magpie/tree/74b30cb7f86763d9e7cc4574477379b939487fd4/internal/gui/assets

The discovery card grid, text size choices, macOS login item, and lightweight
window lifecycle also reference Magpie. The WebKit page zoom and release workarounds are
adapted in internal/desktop/webview_darwin.go.
